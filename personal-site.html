I am a Neuroscience PhD researcher at Stockholm University with a background in biomedical science. I use machine learning methods — including Support Vector Machines and Bayesian mixed-effects models — to decode affective representations from single-subject neuroimaging data.

Research

My work sits at the intersection of affective neuroscience, computational neuroimaging, and machine learning. I use single-subject fMRI designs with high trial-count paradigms to decode and characterise neural representations of emotion, aiming to bridge categorical and dimensional accounts of affect.

fMRI & Neuroimaging

Single-subject fMRI paradigm design, acquisition, and preprocessing using fMRIPrep and SPM12. First- and second-level GLM modelling with custom HRF settings.

Multivariate Pattern Analysis

MVPA with Support Vector Machines (SVM) and cross-validation schemes (LORO-CV). Using PRoNTo and nilearn for decoding emotional states from brain activation patterns.

Emotion Recognition

Investigating the neural substrates of facial and vocal emotion recognition. Combining dimensional (valence/arousal) and categorical emotion models with brain atlas-based ROI analysis.

Bayesian Modelling

Applying Bayesian mixed-effects models (brms/Stan) and Hierarchical Gaussian Filters (HGF) to model individual differences in emotion perception and affective prediction.
